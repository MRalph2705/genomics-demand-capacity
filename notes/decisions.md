# My decisions

Write one short entry for each choice you make. An interviewer will ask "why did you do it that way?"
Use this form: **Choice. Why. What else I could have done.**

## Example (from a different project)
**Choice:** I counted a loan as late only after the due date had passed.
**Why:** a loan that is not due yet cannot be late.
**Other option:** count all open loans. That would make the late rate look better than it is.
